## 1. Introduction

Welcome to Vyavhar Book: Digital Khata, an offline digital ledger application developed by ErrorLabs.

The application helps users maintain customer records, track money given and received, manage outstanding balances, and record event-related cash and gift entries.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the application handles, how it is stored, how it is used, and what choices are available to users.

By using the application, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## 2. Information You Enter

Depending on how you use the application, you may enter the following information:

**Customer information**
- Customer names.
- Phone numbers.
- Village details.
- Relationship details.
- Personal notes.

**Financial and transaction information**
- Transaction amounts.
- Money given and received records.
- Transaction dates.
- Customer balances.
- Transaction descriptions and notes.

**Event information**
- Event names and types.
- Event dates.
- Cash and gift entries.
- Gift descriptions.
- Related notes.

This information is entered by you when using the application's features.

Please avoid entering information that is not necessary for maintaining your records.

## 3. How Your Information Is Used

The information you enter is used to provide the application's functionality, including:

- Creating and managing customer records.
- Recording money given and received.
- Calculating and displaying outstanding balances.
- Maintaining transaction history.
- Managing event-related records.
- Searching and viewing saved information.
- Generating PDF and CSV reports.
- Creating and restoring JSON backups.

Your information is used to operate the application's features and is not sold by ErrorLabs.

## 4. Local Data Storage

Vyavhar Book is designed to store your ledger information locally on your Android device.

The application uses a Room database backed by SQLite to store information in its private application storage.

The database is not separately encrypted at rest. Its protection relies on Android's application sandbox and your device's security settings.

Anyone who gains access to your unlocked device or exported backup files may be able to access information contained in them.

## 5. Manual Backup and Export

The application provides manual backup and export functionality.

**JSON Backup and Restore**

- You can create a JSON backup of your application data.
- You choose where to save the backup file.
- You can restore information from a previously created JSON backup.
- Restoring a backup replaces the existing application database records with the restored records.
- JSON backup files are not encrypted by the application.

**PDF and CSV Export**

The application can generate PDF and CSV files containing relevant ledger information.

These files may contain customer details, transaction information, amounts, and other records depending on the export.

You are responsible for storing, sharing, and protecting exported files.

Once a file is saved or shared outside the application, its handling is subject to the privacy and security practices of the destination service or application.

## 6. Android Automatic Backup

Android automatic backup is enabled for the application.

Depending on your Android version, device configuration, and backup settings, Android may back up eligible application data, potentially including locally stored database information and preferences.

These backups are managed by Android and the backup service configured on your device.

ErrorLabs does not operate or directly access this Android-managed backup storage.

Backup availability, retention, and restoration are subject to Android, device, and account settings.

## 7. Data Collection and Transmission

The application is designed to work offline.

Based on the reviewed version 1.0 implementation, the application does not use an ErrorLabs-operated server or cloud service to collect, store, or synchronize your ledger information.

ErrorLabs does not receive your locally stored ledger records through an application-operated backend.

Android-managed backup services and applications you choose to use when exporting or sharing files operate separately and may process information according to their own privacy policies.

## 8. Sharing of Information

ErrorLabs does not sell your personal or financial ledger information.

The application does not automatically share your customer records or transactions with other users.

Your information may leave the application's private storage when:

- You manually export or share a file.
- You save a backup to a location outside the application.
- Android performs an eligible automatic backup.

You should review the privacy practices of any third-party service or application with which you choose to share your information.

## 9. Advertising, Analytics and Tracking

The reviewed version 1.0 of Vyavhar Book does not intentionally integrate advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

If advertising, analytics, or other third-party services are introduced in future versions, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain the relevant data practices.

## 10. Data Retention and Deletion

Your information remains stored on your device until you delete it or remove the application's data.

The application provides deletion functionality for individual records, including supported customer, transaction, and event-related records.

Some customer records may require associated event or ceremony entries to be deleted before the customer record can be removed.

You can also clear the application's stored data through your Android device settings.

Uninstalling the application normally removes its locally stored application data. However, eligible Android backups may remain available through the configured backup service and could potentially be restored later.

Files that you have exported or shared outside the application are not automatically deleted when you uninstall the application.

The application does not require user account registration, so there is no separate account deletion process.

## 11. Data Security

We take reasonable care in designing the application to store information within Android's private application storage.

However, no electronic storage method or security measure can guarantee absolute protection.

For your protection, we recommend:

- Keeping your Android device secured with a screen lock.
- Not sharing your device with untrusted individuals.
- Storing backup files in secure locations.
- Avoiding sharing exported files containing sensitive financial information with unknown parties.

The application does not separately encrypt its database or manually exported backup files.

## 12. Children's Privacy

Vyavhar Book is a general-purpose digital ledger application and is not specifically designed for children.

The application does not intentionally collect information from children through an online account or server-based registration system.

## 13. Third-Party Services

The application is designed to operate without requiring an ErrorLabs-operated online service.

However, Android operating-system services, device backup services, file managers, and other applications used to save or share exported files may operate independently.

Their privacy practices are governed by their respective providers' policies.

We encourage users to review those policies when using such services.
